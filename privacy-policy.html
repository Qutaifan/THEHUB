Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 1, 2026

At Qutaifan, we take your privacy seriously. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you visit our website.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

  • Usage Data: Pages visited, time spent, clicks, and referral sources (via Google Analytics)
  • Device Information: Browser type, operating system, screen resolution, and IP address
  • Cookies: Small files stored on your device to improve your experience and serve relevant ads
  • Email Address: Only if you voluntarily subscribe to our deal alerts or newsletter

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To analyze site traffic and improve our content
  • To serve personalized advertisements through Google AdSense
  • To send deal alerts and newsletters (only if you opt in)
  • To detect and prevent fraud or abuse

3. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We use cookies and similar technologies to:

  • Remember your preferences
  • Analyze site usage via Google Analytics
  • Serve targeted advertisements via Google AdSense

You can disable cookies in your browser settings, but this may affect site functionality.

Optional Google Analytics is blocked by default. It loads only when Google's consent service reports that analytics storage is allowed or that the consent requirement does not apply. Missing, denied, unknown, or unconfigured consent signals leave Analytics disabled. Google's published consent message manages advertising choices where available; the Privacy choices button reopens that message. If the message is unavailable, that button does not grant consent. Learn how Google uses information from sites that use its services. Browser privacy settings and content blockers provide additional controls.

4. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services that may collect data:

  • Google Analytics: Traffic analysis and user behavior tracking
  • Google AdSense: Displaying targeted advertisements
  • Cloudflare: CDN and security services

5. Data Security

We implement reasonable security measures to protect your data. However, no internet transmission is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction or deletion of your data
  • Opt out of cookies and tracking
  • Unsubscribe from emails at any time

7. Children's Privacy

Our website is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. The latest version will always be posted on this page with the updated date.
