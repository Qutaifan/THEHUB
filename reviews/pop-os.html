Pop!_OS Review (2026) — Honest Take for Free Users

FreeSystem76

Pop!_OS, from System76, is a free, open desktop Linux distribution. It is free to use, with no paywall on the core experience.

Below: what you actually get for free, where the limits sit, and who should skip it.

What it does well

  • Genuinely free; the useful parts are not locked behind a paywall.
  • Practical defaults; it works without a steep learning curve.
  • Actively maintained by System76; Creators is a current highlight.

Pros

  • A separate ISO with NVIDIA drivers preinstalled — still the least painful NVIDIA experience in desktop Linux.
  • Automatic window tiling built in and genuinely usable, without configuring a tiling window manager.
  • System76 builds and sells the hardware, so firmware and driver support on their machines is first-party.
  • Sensible recovery partition and a straightforward upgrade path.

Cons

  • The COSMIC desktop is mid-rewrite in Rust. That is a real transition, and running a desktop through its own rebuild means rough edges and shifting behaviour.
  • Release cadence slipped noticeably while COSMIC was being built — base packages aged in the meantime.
  • A small company's distro. If System76's priorities change, so does Pop!_OS.
  • Ubuntu-based but divergent enough that Ubuntu instructions do not always apply cleanly.

What it costs

Nothing. Pop!_OS is free and there is no paid edition.

System76 funds it by selling laptops and desktops. You are not the customer — their hardware buyers are — which is worth knowing when judging how long the project will stay a priority.

Best alternatives

  • Ubuntu — Pop's base, with broader documentation and vendor support (linux).
  • Fedora Workstation — Newer graphics stack, better for very recent AMD hardware (linux).
  • Nobara — If gaming specifically is the reason you are here (linux).

FAQ

Is Pop!_OS free? Yes — it is free to use.

Do I need a card? No, to start.

Best for? a free, open desktop Linux distribution without paying.
