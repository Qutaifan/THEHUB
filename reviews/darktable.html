darktable Review (2026) — Honest Take for Free Users

Freeopen-sourceOfficial project

darktable, from darktable Team, is a text-to-image generator that turns prompts into pictures. On its Open Source plan it is genuinely usable, not a teaser.

Below: what you actually get for free, where the limits sit, and who should skip it.

What it does well

  • Real image capability on the Open Source tier — no credit card to start.
  • Practical for everyday use; outputs are usable, not just demos.
  • Actively maintained by darktable Team; RAW photo is a current highlight.

Pros

  • No-cost entry with meaningful features.
  • Backed by darktable Team, so it is actively maintained.
  • Good fit for users who want a text-to-image generator that turns prompts into pictures without paid subscriptions.

Cons

  • Support is community forums and issue trackers unless you pay for a support plan.
  • Less specialized than dedicated tools for adjacent tasks.
  • No one owes you a fix on a deadline — roadmap and bug priority are the maintainers' call.

What it costs

darktable is open source, so the core product is free to use and stays that way.

Plenty of open-source projects also sell hosting, support or an enterprise edition alongside the free release — check the project's own pricing page for what, if anything, is sold on top.
